Material Symbols Rounded, filled, 20px SVGs from Google.

The 179 selected icons are bundled as vector PDF image assets for MapNote.
The picker order and stable identifiers are in
`Sources/PasscordMapSymbols/MapNoteSymbol.swift`. Matching white vector
backgrounds are generated by `Scripts/generate_sticker_vectors.swift`.
